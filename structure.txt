# Interview Preparation Guide — Full Stack Developer

## Workspace Structure

### Conventions
- **Folders:** kebab-case (e.g., `array-problems`, `basic-problems`)
- **JS files:** snake_case (e.g., `bubble_sort.js`, `fibonacci_recursion.js`)
- **LeetCode files:** `NNN_title.js` (e.g., `001_two_sum.js`, `189_rotate_array.js`)
